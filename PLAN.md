### E3 - Feature Branches

## A) Geometry:

I will place 120 small triangles around the mouth area/bottom half of the face to make a beard. If it comes out clean, defined, and doesn't cover up the mouth (source of emotion) it would be viable for an icon creator for adding a facial hair option.

## B) Assets:

I plan on placing a picture of crown upon their head, and a cartoony, large mustache as well right above the mouth and below the nose. I suspect I can find simple clip art version via Google quite easily. I want this to make the character look regal and powerful, but still cohesive; I don't want the difference in artstyle to be too jarring.

## C) Color and Curves:

I will use a Bezier curve to represent a frown, turning a smile upside down, and applying a gradient up from cool to warmer/dark red, making it look like the face is very angry. I want this one to look like a simple emote for people to use or display in a potential app.

## Criteria:

I will choose the portrait that most clearly displays its intended purpose, while looking inline with the relatively simplistic style.
